ROUTE BOOK

The pan-India loop

—

A loop from Trivandrum down to Kanyakumari, up the west coast into Kutch, over the Himalaya to Ladakh, east across the Gangetic plain into Nepal, Bhutan and the Northeast, and home down the east coast. It returns to Bangalore and Delhi on the way, and those returns are where a rider can join it or leave it.

This is the route as drawn, not a diary of the one running of it. Every distance and every hour on these pages comes out of the plan file that generated the loop; every rupee comes out of what that ride actually cost.

Where this book has nothing to tell you — riding seasons, permits — it says so in a dashed box and stops. None of it is guessed. Print it, carry it, and fill the blanks in yourself.

MONEY

What it costs

There are no invented rates here. One rider's actual spend across this ride is divided by the nights it took, and those per-night rates are applied to the shape of the loop.

WHEN TO RIDE

Season calendar

A loop this long has no single riding season. It crosses several, and they do not agree with each other — which months work for which sector is a question of research rather than of arithmetic, and nothing in the plan file answers it. The rows below are filled in only where that research has been done and written down.

A dashed box means this book has nothing to tell you about that sector. It does not mean the sector is open, and it is not a shrug — dates and permits are the part of a route book a rider acts on, so a gap is shown as a gap rather than filled with something plausible. Find that information elsewhere, from a source that is current, before you commit to dates.
